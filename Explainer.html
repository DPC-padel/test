Your Rating

How Your
Rating Is Calculated

01

Every Match Generates a Score

Each time you play, the points you score in the match are recorded. In Americano format this is the raw points won. In Tournament format it's win/loss points. These feed directly into your cumulative score.

02

Score Builds Your Rating

Your Rating is derived from your cumulative score adjusted for matches played. It's a decimal number (e.g. 3.4) that reflects your current level. The more you play — and the better you perform — the higher it climbs.

Formula Rating = Total Score ÷ Matches Played
03

Rating Determines Your League

Your rating places you in the right league. As you improve, you move up. Ratings are recalculated after every session so the leaderboard always reflects where you stand right now.

Your Rank

How the Leaderboard
Rank Is Assigned

01

Overall Ranking — From the Sheet

The Ranking tab on each leaderboard shows your official rank. This is calculated centrally based on your score and rating, and is updated after every session. Ties in score are broken by matches played, then alphabetically.

02

Americano Ranking — Calculated Live

The Americano tab ranks players purely by cumulative points scored across all Americano sessions. Players with equal points share the same rank. This updates in real time every time the leaderboard loads.

03

Tournament Ranking — Break Point Only

In Break Point's Tournament tab, ranking is by points earned per match (win = 3 pts, draw = 1 pt, loss = 0 pts). Tiebreakers: wins → matches played → name. Every tournament round counts.

FAQ

Common Questions

How often is the leaderboard updated?

The leaderboard pulls fresh data from our system every hour automatically. Admins can force a refresh immediately after a session. Data is cached for speed — so if you just played, give it up to an hour to reflect.

Does playing more matches always help my rating?

Yes and no. More matches give you more points, but your rating is score per match. Playing badly and losing points can bring your rating down. Consistency and performance both matter.

What happens when two players have the same score?

Tied players share the same rank number. The tiebreaker for display order is matches played (more matches = higher display position), then alphabetical name. But both officially hold the same rank.

Can I move between leagues?

Yes. As your rating improves, you'll be assessed for the next league. Moving up is based on sustained performance, not a single session. The DPC team reviews placements periodically.

Why do I see Score and Rating as separate columns?

Score is your raw cumulative points total. Rating is the normalised decimal value (like 3.4) that accounts for how many matches you've played. Rating is what determines your league tier.

The Americano tab shows different numbers than the Ranking tab — why?

They measure different things. The Ranking tab is your official overall standing based on score + rating calculated centrally. The Americano tab shows only points from Americano-format sessions, ranked live on the leaderboard.
